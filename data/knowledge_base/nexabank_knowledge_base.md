# 1. Institutional Profile and Banking Principles

## 1.1 About NexaBank

NexaBank is a fictional full-service commercial bank designed for a digital banking and agentic AI prototype. The institution offers retail banking, digital payments, cards, consumer financing, customer support, fraud monitoring, and financial guidance.

NexaBank operates through a combination of digital channels, branches, automated teller machines, customer service channels, and controlled internal banking systems. Customer-facing AI services are considered support systems and do not replace authorized banking decision systems.

## 1.2 Core Banking Principles

Customer identity must be verified before protected customer information is disclosed.

Financial transactions must be authorized through the appropriate banking system.

Customer-specific information must not be inferred when it is unavailable.

Bank policy documents are authoritative for product and procedure questions within the prototype.

AI-generated recommendations must be distinguished from formal banking decisions.

Fraud indicators are risk signals and are not, by themselves, proof of fraud.

Customer privacy and least-privilege access apply to all internal and AI-enabled services.

When information is incomplete, the system must disclose the limitation rather than inventing an answer.

# 17. Policy Decision Examples and Edge Cases

## 17.1 Customer Asks for a Balance Without Authentication

The assistant must not disclose a customer-specific balance. It should request authentication through the authorized banking channel or explain that it cannot access protected information.

## 17.2 Customer Has DTI of 52%

The customer falls into the above-50% category and normally requires enhanced review. The AI must not automatically reject the customer unless the authorized credit policy explicitly provides an automatic rejection rule.

## 17.3 Customer Has DTI of 25% but Poor Credit History

Low DTI alone does not establish eligibility. The AI should explain that credit history and other underwriting criteria remain relevant.

## 17.4 Customer Requests PKR 6 Million Personal Financing

The requested amount exceeds the prototype personal financing maximum of PKR 5 million. The AI should explain that the requested amount is outside the documented prototype product range and should not imply that an exception exists.

## 17.5 Customer Reports an Unauthorized Transaction

The AI should prioritize account protection and escalation. It should guide the customer to freeze the relevant card or account where possible and contact the 24-hour fraud hotline.

## 17.6 Customer Asks Why a Transaction Was Flagged

The AI may explain general fraud-monitoring concepts but should not disclose sensitive detection logic, internal thresholds, or confidential monitoring rules.

## 17.7 Policy Document and Customer Record Conflict

For general policy, the current policy document is authoritative. For customer-specific facts, the authorized live or synthetic banking system is authoritative. The AI should not overwrite one with the other.

## 17.8 Static RAG Data and Future Live Data

If the prototype is later connected to live banking services, live authorized data should supersede synthetic customer records for customer-specific questions. Static policy documents should be versioned and updated when policy changes.
