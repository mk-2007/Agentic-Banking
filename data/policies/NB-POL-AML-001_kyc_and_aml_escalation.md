# KYC Completeness and AML Escalation (synthetic prototype policy)

This is a synthetic document for the NexaBank prototype. It is not a real bank policy.

## 1. KYC statuses
Statuses follow the NexaBank knowledge base: Pending, Verified, Restricted, Under Review, Closed.

## 2. Required items
A complete KYC file holds identity document verification, proof of address, and source of funds
where the activity warrants it. KYC must be reviewed at least every 365 days.

## 3. Incomplete KYC with high-value activity
When KYC is Pending, or items are missing, and the account receives or sends a payment of
PKR 300,000 or more:

1. Identify exactly which items are missing.
2. Request the missing documents from the customer.
3. Do not restrict the account on missing documents alone. Restriction requires a separate risk
   indicator and the approvals in NB-POL-AUTH-001.

## 4. Conflicting customer information
If the address or identity details differ between the customer record and KYC documents,
record the mismatch as evidence and request customer verification. A mismatch alone is not
proof of fraud.

## 5. Escalation to compliance
Escalate to a compliance officer when documents are refused, repeatedly not provided, or when
the pattern of activity does not fit the declared source of funds.
